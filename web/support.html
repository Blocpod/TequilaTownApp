Help desk

Support

Something not working, or a question about your passport? Write to us at support@tequilatown.com. During the festival you can also find us at the Mercado.

Common questions

A QR code won't scan

Open the app's Scan tab (not your phone's camera), make sure the whole code is in frame and well lit. If it still fails, show the code printed under the QR to festival staff. Each code only completes the mission step it belongs to.

I can't start the Grand Collector mission

It unlocks after you complete the other ten missions. Your passport shows which ones are left.

My ticket says it's already claimed

A ticket can only be linked to one account. If you bought it for someone else, they should link it in their own app. If you think it was linked by mistake, email us with your order details.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a 6-digit code (valid for 15 minutes). If you signed up with Apple, use Sign in with Apple instead.

The Oracle says I've reached today's limit

To keep things fair for everyone, chats, portraits and bottle scans have daily allowances that refill at midnight UTC.

How do I pick up a Mercado reservation?

Show the pickup code from your order (it looks like TT-7K2Q) at the Mercado and pay there. You can hold up to five reservations at a time and cancel any of them in the app.

How do I hide from the leaderboard?

Open Settings from the Me tab and turn off Show me on the leaderboard. Only your first name and last initial are ever shown.

Delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your Tequila Town account and its data at any time. This applies to the Tequila Town app on iOS and Android, published by [DEVELOPER / LEGAL ENTITY NAME].

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Tequila Town app and sign in.
  2. Open the Me tab and tap the Settings gear.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email support@tequilatown.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Send it from the email address on your account (or, for Sign in with Apple, tell us the email Apple shared with us).
  3. We'll verify the request and delete your account within [30] days, then confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, phone, password hash, profile photo and Sign in with Apple link.
  • Your passport: missions, points, stamps, saved drinks and Agave Persona.
  • Your survey answers (aggregate sponsor statistics are recalculated without them).
  • All La Memoria Viva portraits. Shared links stop working.
  • Mercado reservations, pickup codes, requests and waitlist sign-ups.
  • Your leaderboard entry and every signed-in session.
  • Your linked ticket is released so it can be linked again.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you in our database. Our hosting provider's routine server logs (which can include your IP address and account ID) expire automatically after a limited period [e.g. up to 30 days]. Chat messages and bottle photos are never stored by us; see the Privacy Policy for how our AI providers handle data.
