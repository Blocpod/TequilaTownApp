Privacy Policy

Tequila Town is a festival companion app for guests 21 and over. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to delete it.

"Tequila Town", "we" and "us" refer to [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [ADDRESS]. Questions: support@tequilatown.com.

AI features

The Oracle features use AI models from third-party providers, which we reach through OpenRouter (openrouter.ai). The model providers include [e.g. Anthropic and Google; list the providers configured at launch].

  • El Cantinero & La Mesa (chat): your recent messages in the conversation are sent to the AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store the content of your chats on our servers; we only count how many messages you send each day to apply fair-use limits. Your conversation may be kept on your device by the app.
  • La Memoria Viva (portraits): the selfie you choose is sent to an AI image provider to create a vintage portrait. We do not keep your original selfie. The generated portrait is stored on our servers until you delete it or delete your account. Each portrait has a private, unguessable link; anyone you share that link with can view it.
  • El Ojo (bottle scanner): the photo you take is sent to an AI provider for analysis and is not stored by us.
  • Agave Persona: your Ritual answers (not your name or email) are sent to an AI provider to write your persona.

OpenRouter and the model providers process this data under their own terms and privacy policies and may retain it for a limited time, for example for abuse monitoring. [Confirm the data-retention / zero-retention settings configured in OpenRouter.] Please don't include sensitive personal information in chats or photos.

Who we share data with

  • Service providers that run the app for us: Netlify (hosting and data storage, United States), OpenRouter and the AI model providers listed above, Resend (sending password-reset emails, if you request one), and Apple (Sign in with Apple).
  • Ticketing: when ticket validation is enabled, the ticket code you enter is checked with our ticketing provider [e.g. Tixr].
  • Sponsors: aggregate, de-identified survey statistics only.
  • Other guests: your leaderboard name and stats (if you leave it on), and any portrait link you choose to share.
  • Legal: when required by law or to protect the safety of guests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Password-reset codes expire after 15 minutes and are stored only as a hash. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period [e.g. up to 30 days].

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (open Settings from the Me tab, then tap Delete account) or by emailing support@tequilatown.com from the address on your account. Deletion immediately and permanently erases your account, progress and points, survey answers, avatar, portraits, shop reservations and requests, leaderboard entry and active sessions, and releases any ticket you linked so it can be claimed again. Aggregate survey statistics are updated to remove your answers. See Support for step-by-step instructions.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, phone and photo in the app, and hide yourself from the leaderboard at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@tequilatown.com.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights. [Add jurisdiction-specific disclosures as advised by counsel.]

Age requirement

Tequila Town is only for people 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sign-in sessions use short-lived tokens, and refresh tokens are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

Changes

If we make material changes we will update the date above and let you know in the app.
